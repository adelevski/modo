How this works

Teal points are road vertices within 60 seconds of the shortest possible longest trip, not venues or safe stops. Routes may omit road curves. Pins stay at confirmed coordinates; dotted segments show road-vertex snaps. Results use a static Chicago-area snapshot without traffic.
